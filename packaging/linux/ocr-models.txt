Text recognition models used by Select text on Linux.

text-detection.onnx and text-recognition.onnx are the pre-trained models of the ocrs project
by Robert Knight (https://github.com/robertknight/ocrs, models at
https://huggingface.co/robertknight/ocrs), trained on HierText and synthetic data.
